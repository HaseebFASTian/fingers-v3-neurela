## Main image prompt

Use case: ads-marketing. Create a premium photorealistic 3D food cutout for the central animated hero of Neurela, a cheese brand. Single sculptural arrangement, isolated on a truly transparent background. A generous golden cheddar wedge at the top tilted slightly backwards, several soft-edged cheddar slices layered under it, an ivory mozzarella block behind to the right, a few mozzarella cubes near the bottom, and a small loose nest of fresh shredded mozzarella at the base. The composition should feel like a beautiful layered cheese sculpture, approximately as tall as wide, compact and appetising, not a scattered assortment. Three-quarter front view, natural ivory and rich cheese-gold colours, realistic porous cheddar and milky mozzarella textures, soft studio highlights from upper left, convincing depth and soft contact shadows between pieces. Centre the whole composition with 8 percent clear margin, fully visible silhouette. No board, plate, table, packaging, person, basil, tomato, text, label, logo, watermarks or background. This is a real-looking luxury food advertisement asset, not cartoon cheese and not a flat illustration.

## Detail image prompt

Use case: product-mockup. A small photorealistic cheese still-life cutout for Neurela website hero detail card: three thin golden cheddar slices gracefully curled and folded at the edge, resting against one small rectangular cheddar piece, with four ivory mozzarella cubes grouped closely alongside. Three-quarter angle, compact arrangement, rich appetising gold and ivory, realistic soft cheese texture, beautiful studio light from top left. Genuine transparent background, isolated full object visible, generous clear margins, no plate or board, no text, no branding, no props, no watermark. Premium real food photography with depth, not illustration.

## Behaviour

Scoped hero.css loads after mobile.css. hero.js adds input-driven pointer tilt only for fine hover devices. A single 1.4-second CSS entrance settles into a static composition. Reduced-motion disables both effects. Side annotations and the Pause control were removed at the client's request. Catalogue actions retain their existing destinations.
