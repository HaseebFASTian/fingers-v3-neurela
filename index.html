What is in the Neurela range?

Our range includes shredded mozzarella, mozzarella and cheddar blends, cheese dice, noodle-cut mozzarella, burger and cheddar slices, plus cheddar and mozzarella blocks.

Which pack sizes are available?

Shredded blends come in 2 kg pouches. Shredded mozzarella, dice, noodle cut and blocks have 1 kg and 2 kg options. Burger and cheddar slices come in 200 g and 1 kg packs. Open a product to see its available formats.

Can I enquire for my restaurant or shop?

Yes. Save the products and packs you need, then prepare a business enquiry with your business type, city and requirements.

Can I order and pay on this website?

This website showcases our cheese range and helps you prepare an enquiry. Online ordering and payment are not available. The current enquiry form creates a preview that you can download; it does not send a message.

Where can I check ingredients and storage details?

Refer to the label on your product for ingredients, allergens, storage instructions and shelf life. For further product information, include your question and the exact product in your enquiry.
